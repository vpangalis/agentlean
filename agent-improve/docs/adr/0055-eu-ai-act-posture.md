# 0055. EU AI Act posture: a limited-risk assistant with a human decision

**Status:** PROPOSED (architecture sort, 2026-09-26)

**Source:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) §67

## Context

The product gives advice inside a regulated employer's improvement process.

## Decision

The Belt and champion make every decision; the system discloses that it is AI and keeps the audit trail.

## Consequences

Human approval at every gate is a compliance requirement, not only a design choice.

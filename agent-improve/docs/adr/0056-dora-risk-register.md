# 0056. Operational risks are kept in a DORA-structured register

**Status:** PROPOSED (architecture sort, 2026-09-26)

**Source:** [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) §68

## Context

Financial-sector customers ask for DORA-style evidence.

## Decision

Risks are recorded in the DORA structure, with owner and treatment.

## Consequences

Deferred items (e.g. geographic redundancy, 0045) are tracked there.
